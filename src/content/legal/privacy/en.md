## Who we are

This website is operated by Sunrise Villa, a 3-star guesthouse at Str. Mihai Eminescu 41, 417495 Sânmartin, Băile Felix, Bihor County, Romania. The website address is https://sunrisevilla-felix.com. For any privacy question, you can reach us at contact@sunrisevilla-felix.com or on +40 771 734 907.

## What personal data we collect and why

**Contact form.** When you use the contact form on this site, we collect your name, email address, phone number, the dates you are interested in and your message. We use this data only to answer your enquiry about a stay or other information about Sunrise Villa. We do not use it for marketing. Contact form data is kept for 12 months and then deleted.

## Cookies

Our website does not use cookies. Fonts and images are hosted on our own server, with no external tracking or analytics services. The one exception is the Google map in the page footer, described below.

## Google map and links to other websites

The Google map in the page footer only loads after you press "Show Google map". Only then does your browser connect to Google, which receives your IP address and may set cookies, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Without that click, nothing is sent to Google.

The booking buttons lead to Booking.com, and "Open in Google Maps" and "Directions" open Google Maps. Once you are on those sites, their own privacy policies apply.

## Who we share your data with

Personal data collected through the contact form is sent through our web host's (ClausWeb) email server to our email address so that we can reply. We do not share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising.

## How long we keep your data

Contact form data is kept for 12 months.

## Your rights over your data

You can ask at any time what personal data we hold about you. You can also ask us to delete any personal data. For such requests, contact us at contact@sunrisevilla-felix.com.

## Where your data is sent

Contact form data is processed on ClausWeb servers located in Romania.

## How we protect your data

Our website uses an SSL certificate (HTTPS) to encrypt the communication between your browser and our server.

## Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy. Any change will be published on this page.
